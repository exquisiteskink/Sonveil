# Sonveil 1.3.13

Seek bar, mini-player progress, Settings buttons, sliders, switches, selected navigation, and action buttons now follow a single Material 3 scheme derived from the playing album art. One seed is chosen from the artwork; tones are contrast-checked against the surfaces they sit on. When nothing is playing, the app falls back to the static scheme (gold in dark mode).

Install `Sonveil-1.3.13.apk` on Android 8.0 or later. The APK uses the existing signing certificate and upgrades Sonveil 1.3.8–1.3.12 in place.

Validation: 123 unit tests passed; Android lint reports no errors. On-device visuals were not checked (no phone attached). A connected car and external Poweramp EQ/Wavelet were not tested.

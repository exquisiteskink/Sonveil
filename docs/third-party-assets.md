# Third-party assets

`app/src/main/assets/autoeq.json.gz` contains headphone equalizer presets derived from
[AutoEq by Jaakko Pasanen](https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq). AutoEq is distributed
under the MIT license; the full notice is bundled in the APK at
[`app/src/main/assets/licenses/AutoEq-MIT.txt`](../app/src/main/assets/licenses/AutoEq-MIT.txt).
The compressed JSON is data, not a precompiled library or executable.

App artwork, screenshots, and branding are maintained in this repository. Music album and
artist images shown at runtime come from the user's connected server. Third-party library
licenses are supplied by their respective Maven artifacts.
